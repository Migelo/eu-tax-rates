EU Personal Income Tax + Social Contributions

Effective tax rate by gross annual income for all 27 EU member states (2025/2026) — single employee, no children

Methodology

  • Effective rate = (income tax + social contributions) / gross income
  • Income tax is computed on taxable income (gross minus standard deduction, minus social contributions where deductible)
  • Surcharges on tax (e.g. Belgium municipal 7%, Germany solidarity 5.5%) are applied to the computed tax
  • Surtax brackets (Portugal, Poland, France) are computed separately on gross income
  • Social contributions with floors (France T2, Ireland USC) only apply to income within the floor-cap range
  • Germany's linear-progressive zones use average rate approximation

Assumptions & Limitations

  • Single/unmarried employee, no children, no itemized deductions
  • Employment income only (no capital, self-employment)
  • No church tax (DE/FI/DK), no regional variations (ES/IT use national average)
  • Non-EUR amounts converted at approximate April 2026 rates
  • Netherlands Box 1 brackets include embedded national insurance premiums
  • Sweden employee pension (7%) is fully creditable against tax, modeled as 0%
  • Data sourced from PwC Tax Summaries, national tax authorities, Tax Foundation
